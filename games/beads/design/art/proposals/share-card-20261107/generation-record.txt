分享卡配图生成史 —— WXG-T-590（2026-11-07）
================================================================

用途：微信小游戏「分享卡片」配图（结算面板 share → wx.shareAppMessage.imageUrl）
规格：5:4 横构图；不透明（分享卡不走透明）；⛔ 不含文字（标题由卡片文字区渲染）

通道与模型
----------------------------------------------------------------
云端 阿里云百炼 DashScope · 按量付费通道（maas，key = .env.local: DASHSCOPE_API_KEY）
模型 qwen-image-3.0（0.18 元/张）
脚本 games/beads/design/ai-workflows/dashscope-generate.py
命令模板：
  python3 games/beads/design/ai-workflows/dashscope-generate.py \
    --model qwen-image-3.0 --size '1280*1024' --seed <seed> --text "<prompt>"
⚠ 同模型连跑会覆盖 temp/dashscope-out/custom/qwen_image_30.png ⇒ 每次调用后立刻 mv

四次调用（4 张，零浪费）
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. seed 41 → s41.png
   prompt: A top-down close-up of a finished fuse-bead artwork: bright round plastic beads
           with center holes, arranged into a full colorful pattern on a clear square pegboard,
           warm cream paper background (#F1E8D8), sliver of wooden desk at the edge, soft
           daylight from upper left, flat vector illustration, no text, wide horizontal 5:4
   判：实物俯拍感重、珠阵密到不可辨、配色像噪点 ⇒ 缩略图场景不可用，弃

2. seed 52 → s52.png
   prompt: Extreme close-up of a small finished fuse-bead shape: a bright coral-red heart made
           of large round plastic beads with visible center holes, only about 12 beads across
           the frame, generous empty cream space around the heart, no text, 5:4
   判：单色心形、干净、构图为缩略图友好 ⇒ 备选（色相单一，与「多色拼豆」关联弱）

3. seed 63 → s63.png
   prompt: … four pastel colors … a few loose colorful beads scattered beside it …
   判：多色达成但心形未成形、散珠抢焦点 ⇒ 弃

4. seed 74 → s74.png  ★ 定稿
   prompt: Flat vector illustration: a heart silhouette completely filled by large round fuse
           beads, four pastel colors in a simple checker pattern (coral red, warm yellow,
           soft mint, pale sky blue), every bead touching its neighbours so the heart outline
           reads perfectly, no loose or scattered beads anywhere, centered on warm cream paper
           background (#F1E8D8), soft short shadow, thick clean outlines, flat color fills,
           no text, no letters, no watermark
   判：四色拼满心形、轮廓清晰、平涂干净、无文字无 watermark ⇒ 定稿

定稿 → 发布件
----------------------------------------------------------------
正本   design/art/proposals/share-card-20261107/s74.png   1280×1024 RGB
发布件 games/beads/share/share-card.jpg   640×512 baseline JPEG q85 = 48.2 KB
  PIL 步骤：Image.open(s74).convert('RGB').resize((640,512), LANCZOS)
            .save(quality=85, optimize=True)   # baseline，非 progressive
  ⛔ 不用 PNG：同图 PNG-P256 = 178.8 KB；分享卡不需要透明 ⇒ JPEG 体积为 1/3.7
  ⛔ 不用 progressive：微信各版本解码兼容面按 baseline 最稳

落位与接线
----------------------------------------------------------------
构建  tools/scripts/build-cocos.mjs::stageShareAssets（约定 games/<game>/share/** ⇒ 产物根同名文件）
      为什么不能直接放 cocos/assets/：依赖分析只认 .prefab/.scene/.mtl 引用，运行期字符串路径
      不算引用 ⇒ 会被剔除或落进 uuid 分桶（同 check:cocos-mask 的「存在≠进包」墙）
代码  effects-dispatch.ts::fireShare → share({ title: COPY_TOKENS.share_title, imageUrl: 'share-card.jpg' })
取证  产物 games/beads/cocos/build/wechatgame/share-card.jpg = 49365 字节
      主包 2638.4 → 2686.7 KB（137 文件），距 4096 红线余量 1409.3 KB
